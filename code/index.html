How It Works

The Commitment-First Philosophy

Having thousands of ROMs at your fingertips leads to choice paralysis. ORGL breaks the endless browsing loop with a 3-step ritual.

STEP 01

Pick One Game

Choose a title from "Tonight's Trio" suggestions, filter by system, or search your library.

STEP 02

Commit

That game locks in. ORGL hides the rest of your library — no switching until you finish or release it.

STEP 03

Finish or Drop

Mark it done or move on with peace of mind. Log your run card and pick your next adventure.

"Every run teaches you something."

Built for Retro Hardware

Features Designed Around You

ORGL stays lightweight, fast, and completely out of the way of your existing emulator setups.

Wishlist Play Queue

Keep a focused wishlist separate from general favorites. ORGL uses it to feed "Tonight's trio" suggestions when you're ready to pick next.

Your Choice of Emulator

Use RetroArch cores or standalone apps (DuckStation, Dolphin, PPSSPP, melonDS, etc.). Configure launcher actions per system or per game.

ES-DE Compatible

Directly reuses your scraped media, cover art, and metadata from EmulationStation-DE. Zero re-scraping needed, completely read-only.

RetroAchievements

ORGL tracks your achievement progress directly. Unlocks happen inside RetroArch while ORGL keeps your active record in sync.

HowLongToBeat Estimates

Know how long a title takes before committing. View average hours for Main Story, Main + Extra, and Completionist runs.

Play Journal & Run Cards

Keep a personal log of finished and dropped runs with ratings, custom review notes, completion dates, and shareable social collages.

Gamepad-First UI

Designed from day one for couch and handheld gaming. Full D-pad navigation, custom button legends, and zero touch requirements.

Your Files, Your Rules

ROMs stay untouched and read-only. ORGL never alters game files. All app configuration lives cleanly in a portable local ORGL directory.
